Buying guide

When should a small team automate security questionnaires?

Automation is useful when you have reviewed answers worth reusing. If the evidence is missing, generating more sentences creates more work for the reviewer.

Start with the work that repeats

Look at your recent buyer questionnaires. Mark the questions that ask about the same controls in different words. Collect the approved answers and their evidence references. This is useful even if you keep answering in a spreadsheet.

A readiness checklist

  • You have statements that your security owner has approved.
  • You know which product and environment each statement covers.
  • You can name an owner for unanswered questions and expired statements.
  • You need to return the buyer's own spreadsheet.
  • You want reviewers to see which approved answer was used.

If the first three items are missing, build the library and assign owners first. The free CSV template gives you a starting point without buying software.

What Questionnaire Autopilot does

The app compares questions with your approved answers, copies matches with evidence references and sets aside questions needing a person. You can review answers together with their evidence and check the final file before sending it.

It runs on your computer. A local model is optional. If you choose a cloud model, the app sends the question and up to three matching approved answers only after you allow it. The cloud provider then handles that data under its own terms.

What still needs your team

The app does not create missing security controls, award certifications or approve contractual commitments. A person still owns the evidence, reviews exceptions and decides what to send. See questions to route to an owner.
