SIG Lite vs CAIQ: what small software vendors should know

A buyer may send SIG Lite, CAIQ or a custom security questionnaire. Check the publisher and edition before preparing your response; the formats cover different scopes of risk.

Who publishes them

SIG (Standardized Information Gathering) questionnaires are published by Shared Assessments, a membership organisation. SIG is available through membership or a separate license. SIG Lite is intended for an initial or lower-risk review. See Shared Assessments’ description of SIG and its SIG FAQ.

CAIQ (Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire) is published by the Cloud Security Alliance. It accompanies the CSA Cloud Controls Matrix and uses yes/no questions to assess cloud security controls. CSA provides the CCM and CAIQ v4.1 download. Check the edition of the file your buyer supplied rather than assuming a question count.

How they differ in practice

SIG LiteCAIQ
PublisherShared AssessmentsCloud Security Alliance
Cost to obtainLicensedFree download
FocusBroad third-party risk, not only cloudCloud service controls
Typical useInitial or lower-risk vendor reviewCloud providers, often published proactively
Answer styleYes / no / N/A with commentsYes / no / N/A with comments

Question counts change between versions, so check the edition your buyer sent rather than relying on a number from an article.

Which one should you prepare first?

Prepare whichever your buyers actually send. If you sell a cloud product and nobody has asked yet, a completed CAIQ can help structure a cloud security response. First confirm which format your buyers accept and check the publisher’s terms for your intended use.

Answer once, reuse everywhere

The controls behind both questionnaires overlap heavily: access control, encryption, logging, vulnerability management, incident response, business continuity, vendor management and personnel security. Write one approved answer per control in an answer library, with the evidence behind it, so you can reuse a supported statement when a question has the same scope. Review the buyer’s wording each time; similar questions do not always ask for the same commitment.

A note on copyright

Do not redistribute SIG content you do not have a license for. Answer the copy your customer sends you, and keep your library in your own words.
