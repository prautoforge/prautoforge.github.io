Security questionnaire answer library: free CSV template

An answer library is a single spreadsheet of security answers your company has already approved. It turns every questionnaire after the first into mostly copying, and it makes sure the answers you send stay consistent and current.

Download the free template (CSV) ↓

The columns

ColumnWhat goes in it
idA stable ID such as ENC-001. Never reuse an ID for a different answer.
topicEncryption, Access control, Incident response and so on.
questionThe usual wording of the question this answers.
answerThe exact text that was approved, word for word.
evidence_refWhere the proof lives: a policy section, audit report or ticket.
approved_by, approved_atWho signed off and when.
expires_atWhen the answer must be re-checked, usually a year later.
keywordsOther words people use for the same thing (MFA, 2FA, SSO).

How to fill it in

  1. Collect the last two or three questionnaires your company sent, plus your security policies.
  2. For each answer that was reviewed before it was sent, add one row. Keep the approved wording.
  3. Add the evidence reference. If you cannot name the evidence, the answer needs review before it goes in.
  4. Set an expiry date. Controls change, and an out-of-date answer is worse than none.
  5. Ask your security owner to review the whole library once, then add new answers as they are approved.

Most small teams end up with 60 to 120 answers. That covers the large majority of what standard questionnaires such as SIG Lite and CAIQ ask.

Keep it honest

Only put answers in the library that are true today and supported by evidence. If you are not certified to a standard, the approved answer says so. A library of accurate answers is what lets you respond quickly without creating risk.
