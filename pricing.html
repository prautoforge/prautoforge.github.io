Before you decide

Can I use it without AI?

Yes. Matching, checking, gap lists and legal routing work without a model. Optional drafting and chat require a configured local or cloud model.

Can it answer without a library?

You need approved answers for matching and drafting. Start with the CSV template or use Library Builder to prepare existing answers for approval.

Does it include questionnaire standards or certification?

It does not include licensed SIG or CAIQ content, certify your organization or provide legal advice. Supply the questionnaire you have permission to use, and review every response.

Can my team use it together?

Yes. Keep the program in a folder your team shares (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox or a network drive). Saves are locked so two people never overwrite each other, every change is logged with a name, and My work shows each person what is assigned to them. It is not a hosted web app with live co-editing; one license key covers three computers.

How many computers can use one license?

Up to three at a time. Each computer is activated with the license key from your receipt. To move the license to a new computer, open License in the app on the old computer and choose Deactivate this computer, then activate the new one.

What if a computer is replaced or lost?

Email contactprautof@gmail.com with your order number. We can free the activation of a computer you no longer have.
