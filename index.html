What you get

A reviewed first draft in about an hour, not a lost day.

Reuse, don't retype

Questions that match an approved answer get that answer copied exactly, with its evidence ID and document reference.

A clear review queue

Legal questions come first, then gaps, then drafts. Your reviewer sees what needs judgment and skips what doesn't.

A to-do list for gaps

Anything your library can't answer goes into a missing-evidence list for your security owner. Approve it once and it's reused next time.

How it works

Three steps, all on your machine.

Standard Python 3.10+ on Windows, macOS or Linux. No account, no server, no telemetry.

  1. Build your approved-answer library

    One row per answer your team has signed off on: the text, the evidence reference, who approved it and when it expires. Most teams start from their last two questionnaires.

  2. Run the incoming questionnaire

    CSV or XLSX, any column layout with a question column.

    python -m sqautopilot run incoming.xlsx --library answers.csv

  3. Review and send

    You get a draft spreadsheet, a prioritised review queue, a missing-evidence list and an offline HTML report. Nothing is ever submitted for you.

Built-in limits

What it will never do.

A questionnaire answer is a statement about your company. The tool is designed to keep unsupported statements out of it.

  • Invent certifications or audits. A draft that mentions SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, encryption strength, numbers or incident history is discarded unless your cited approved answer says the same thing.
  • Cite evidence that doesn't exist. Every draft must point to real IDs in your library.
  • Send your data anywhere. The optional AI model must run on the same computer. Other addresses are refused in code.
  • Answer legal or contract questions. Indemnity, liability and interpretation of law go straight to a person.
  • Submit anything. You review, you send.
Requirements

What you need.

  • Python 3.10 or newer. The core uses only the standard library.
  • Optional: openpyxl for XLSX files. Without it, use CSV.
  • Optional: a local llama.cpp server and a model you choose, for drafting. Without one, matching and review still work. No model is included.

Questions

FAQ

Does it include SIG or CAIQ questionnaire content?
No. Those are published by their owners under their own terms. Load the questionnaire your customer sent you; the tool works with any spreadsheet that has a question column.
Do I need an AI model?
No. Matching, gap lists, legal routing and the review queue work without one. A local model only adds DRAFTED answers for partial matches, and those are checked against your evidence before you see them.
Does anything get uploaded?
No. The tool collects no usage data and never contacts us. Your purchase is handled by Lemon Squeezy under its own privacy policy.
Is this legal or compliance advice?
No. It's a drafting aid. You remain responsible for every answer you send.
