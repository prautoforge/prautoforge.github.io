        Questions

        Before you buy.

        What makes this different from questionnaire AI?

        It answers only from statements your team approved, and it checks the result. Drafts that add a certification, change a number, drop a “not” or widen the scope are discarded, and the pre-send check applies the same rules to the final file your team edited. It also keeps owners, scope and expiry dates with every approved answer.

        Why not use a full GRC platform?

        Use one when you need continuous control monitoring, audit workflows and a multi-user compliance program. This is for a smaller team that needs to get through customer assurance now without another hosted system, sales call or recurring platform bill.

        Do I need to be technical?

        You need Python 3.10 or newer, then you can open the local Workbench and choose files with ordinary file pickers. The quickstart also documents commands for automation and troubleshooting.

        Do I need an AI model?

        No. Matching, gap lists, legal routing and the review report all work without one. A local model only adds drafts for partial matches, and every draft is checked against your evidence.

        Does anything get uploaded?

        No. The software collects no usage data and never contacts us. Your purchase is handled by Lemon Squeezy under its own privacy policy.

        Does it include SIG or CAIQ content?

        No. Those are published by their owners under their own terms. Use the questionnaire your customer sent you; any spreadsheet with a question column works.

        Is this legal or compliance advice?

        No. It is a drafting aid. You stay responsible for every answer you send.

        What if it doesn't work on my computer?

        Email us within 14 days with your operating system and the error. If we can't get it working, you get a full refund.
