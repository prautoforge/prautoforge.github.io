Review and edit

See the answer and the evidence together.

The review screen keeps each question, proposed response and evidence in one place. The claim guard checks edits for changes to supported claims, including numbers, certifications, negation and scope.

These checks help identify problems. They do not verify your underlying evidence or replace a responsible reviewer.
