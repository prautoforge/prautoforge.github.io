Privacy

PrautoForge sells downloadable software. This page explains what information is involved and who handles it.

The software

Questionnaire Autopilot runs only on your own computer. It collects no usage data, has no analytics, and never contacts PrautoForge. Your questionnaires, approved answers and outputs stay on your machine. If you connect an optional local AI model, the software only accepts a model address on the same computer.

Purchases

Orders are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as reseller and merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email, billing details and payment information under its own privacy policy. PrautoForge never sees your full card details. We receive the order information Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers, such as your name, email address, country and what you bought, and use it only to provide the product, support and refunds, and to keep required business and tax records.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may log technical data such as IP addresses under their own policies. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to answer it. We don't send marketing email and don't sell or share your information.

Your requests

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted where the law allows, write to contactprautof@gmail.com.
