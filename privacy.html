Privacy, in plain language.

Questionnaire Autopilot is local software, not a hosted service. Your questionnaire files stay on the computer where you run it.

The short version: the software has no account, analytics, advertising, or telemetry. This website sets no cookies.

Questionnaire Autopilot

The software processes your questionnaires, approved-answer library, and reports on your own computer. It does not contact PrautoForge or send us usage data. If you choose to use optional AI drafting, the software accepts only a model address on that same computer.

This website

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, or cookies, and the pages do not load fonts or scripts from third parties. GitHub may process basic connection information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy terms to operate the hosting service.

Purchases

When checkout becomes available, orders will be processed by Lemon Squeezy as our reseller and merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy handles billing details and payment information under its own privacy policy. PrautoForge does not receive your full card details. We may receive order details such as your name, email address, country, and product purchased so we can deliver the product, provide support, process refunds, and maintain required business and tax records.

Support email

If you email us, we use the message and your contact details to answer your request and keep a reasonable support record. We do not add you to a marketing list or sell your information.
