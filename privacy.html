Privacy.

Questionnaire Autopilot is local software, not a hosted service. Core file processing runs on your computer. Optional cloud AI and web search send selected content to the services you enable.

Accounts and tracking: the software has no account, analytics, advertising, or telemetry. Its only regular connection is the license check described below. This website sets no cookies.

Questionnaire Autopilot

The software processes your questionnaires, approved-answer library, and reports on your own computer. It does not contact PrautoForge or send us usage data. Questionnaires, libraries, reports, and file names never leave your computer for licensing. Without AI, or with a model running on the same computer, questionnaire processing does not require a cloud model provider. If you enable cloud AI, questions and selected approved answers are sent to your chosen provider after confirmation. Intellinaire chat can also send conversation context and attached content to the selected model service. Web search sends search queries to the configured search service. Those providers process content under their own terms.

Automated checks connect only after you set them up. A domain check sends DNS lookups for the domains you add to Cloudflare’s DNS service and connects to those domains’ own websites; a GitHub check reads repository settings from GitHub with the read-only token you save; both then repeat once a week while the app runs. Identity provider and device checks read export files on your computer and send nothing. Updating the known exploited vulnerabilities list downloads CISA’s public catalogue. If you keep the program in a shared or synced folder, that folder’s service stores the program, its evidence files and the activity log under its own terms.

License activation

To activate the software and to confirm the license, at most once every seven days while the computer is online, the software sends the license key and a label for the computer, such as “Windows computer 3F2A9C”, to the Lemon Squeezy license service. The label is derived from the operating system’s identifier for the computer and does not reveal it. The service returns the status of the key, the number of activations in use, and the name and email address on the order. That information is stored on the computer with the activation. Lemon Squeezy processes this data under its own privacy policy. Deactivating a computer in the License panel removes its activation from the service and deletes the stored license from the computer.

This website

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, or cookies, and the pages do not load fonts or scripts from third parties. The appearance switch stores your light or dark preference in your browser’s local storage. You can clear this through your browser settings. GitHub may process basic connection information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy terms to operate the hosting service.

Purchases

Orders are processed by Lemon Squeezy as our reseller and merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy handles billing details and payment information under its own privacy policy. PrautoForge does not receive your full card details. We may receive order details such as your name, email address, country, and product purchased so we can deliver the product, provide support, process refunds, and maintain required business and tax records.

Support email

If you email us, we use the message and your contact details to answer your request and keep a reasonable support record. We do not add you to a marketing list or sell your information.
