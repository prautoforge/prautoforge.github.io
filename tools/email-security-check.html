Email security check

Enter a domain to see whether others can send email that pretends to come from it. You get a grade from A to F and the exact records to fix. It runs in your browser; nothing is stored.

What this checks

CheckWhy it matters
SPFLists the servers allowed to send mail for the domain. Without it, anyone can claim to send as you.
DMARCTells receivers what to do with mail that fails SPF and DKIM. Only p=quarantine or p=reject stops spoofed mail.
DKIMSigns each message, so receivers can tell it was not changed and came from your provider.
MTA-STSMakes other servers deliver mail to you over an encrypted connection only.
DNSSECSigns your DNS answers, so they cannot be forged on the way.
CAALimits which certificate authorities may issue certificates for your domain.

How the grade works

Every domain starts at 100. Each problem takes points off by how much it matters: a missing SPF or DMARC record on a domain that receives mail costs 15 points, a weak DMARC policy 10, a missing MTA-STS, DNSSEC or CAA record 5. A is 90 or more, B 80, C 70, D 60, and anything lower is F. DKIM is checked under the selectors most providers use; if yours uses another name, the check says so rather than marking it wrong.

Why buyers ask about this

Security questionnaires from customers often ask whether you publish SPF, DKIM and DMARC, because phishing that uses a supplier's domain is a common way into a company. A grade of A or B, with the date you checked, is an easy answer to give.

Privacy

The lookups go from your browser to Cloudflare's public DNS resolver (cloudflare-dns.com), the same public records anyone can read. PrautoForge does not receive the domain or the result.
